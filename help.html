Help and privacy

Before you tap.

Answers for shop owners and for anyone trying the demo.

Product questions

Which phones can tap?

Use a phone that supports NFC tag reading. Unlock the phone, switch on NFC if needed, hold it near the tag and open the notification. A Google account is needed to post a Google review.

Who sets up the review link?

TapRank plans to set up and test each tag using the shop’s own Google review link before dispatch.

What about delivery, returns and support?

UK delivery is included in the proposed price. Delivery dates, a support email and the returns process will be published before sales open. This demo takes no orders.

Whom should I invite to review?

Invite all customers equally. Let them choose their own rating and words. Never offer discounts or gifts for reviews.

Privacy and cookies

This demo uses sample shop details. It does not collect names, addresses, card details or reviews. A preview stays on this page and clears on reload.

TapRank sets no cookies and uses no analytics or contact form. GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security.
